# Teams

A **team** is a shared lmthing workspace that several people use with their own accounts.

**It's its own thing, not a share of someone's.** A team gets its own runtime, its own subscription
tier, and its own credentials — its own LLM budget, its own Slack/Google/GitHub connections. Nothing
is billed to a member, and no member's personal keys are involved.

**It holds shared work.** Projects and spaces live in the team, not in a personal workspace. Members
open them from their own account.

**It has a chat surface.** A Slack-like interface where members talk to each other in channels — and
call THING when they need it. THING answers in the thread, with the team's projects and spaces
available to it, and remembers the conversation across messages.

**Membership has two levels:**

- **Viewer** — use the team's spaces and apps, read the projects, chat with THING. Changes nothing.
- **Editor** — everything a viewer can do, plus editing projects and spaces and configuring the team.

So: a team is a place where a group talks, keeps its shared projects and apps, and has an agent that
works on them — paid for by the team rather than by whoever happens to be typing.
