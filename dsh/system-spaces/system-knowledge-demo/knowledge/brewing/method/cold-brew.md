# Cold brew

Steeped, never heated: **100 g** of coarse coffee to **1 litre** of cold water, **16 hours** in the
walk-in, then filtered once through a paper cone. Served over ice, diluted 1:1 with water.

This aspect deliberately carries NO frontmatter — plain markdown is legal for an option file, and
resolving it must return the raw body rather than a structured object.
