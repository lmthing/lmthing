# Espresso

- **20 g** in, **40 g** out (a 1:2 ratio), **27 seconds**, group at **93 °C**.
- Fine grind, distributed and tamped level.

A shot that runs in under 22 seconds is ground too coarse and gets thrown away rather than served.
