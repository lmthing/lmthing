# Pour-over (the house method)

- Dose **18 g** of coffee, medium grind.
- **300 g** of water at **94 °C**, in four pours: 60 g bloom, then three 80 g pours.
- Total brew time **3:00**, give or take 10 seconds.

If the drawdown finishes before 2:30 the grind was too coarse; past 3:30 it was too fine. The bar
adjusts the grind, never the dose.
